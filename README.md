story (a drone strike on the Public Investment Fund tower in Riyadh, the second
in a week), nine other fronts at a glance, five stories each from Europe, Asia
and the Americas, a world map of the eighteen places the day happened, and the
markets and numbers behind it. The picture is a 1024×1024 PNG drawn as a
newspaper front page, and `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
